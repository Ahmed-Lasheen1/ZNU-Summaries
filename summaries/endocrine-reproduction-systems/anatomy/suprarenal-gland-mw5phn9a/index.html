At a glance

The suprarenal gland is a paired endocrine gland with a cortex and a medulla, sitting in front of the upper pole of each kidney.

Structure and hormones

The suprarenal (adrenal) gland is a bilateral endocrine gland. On cut section it has an outer cortex and an inner medulla.

Zona glomerulosa
Aldosterone
Zona fasciculata
Cortisol
Zona reticularis
Sex hormones
Medulla
Catecholamines (adrenaline and noradrenaline)

Site, shape and capsule

  • Site: epigastrium, in front of the upper pole of each kidney.
  • Weight: 4-5 gm.
  • Shape: right gland pyramidal; left gland semilunar (crescentic).
  • Capsule: a strong fibrous capsule; the gland lies in its own compartment of the renal fascia (Gerota's fascia).

Relations

Right gland

  • Anterior: small area covered by peritoneum of the liver
  • Medially: IVC
  • Laterally: right lobe of the liver
  • Posterior: upper pole of right kidney (does not reach the hilum) and right crus of diaphragm
  • Medially: right celiac ganglion

Left gland

  • Anterior: large area covered by peritoneum of the lesser sac
  • Above: lesser sac and posterior surface of stomach; middle: splenic artery; below: pancreas
  • Posterior: upper pole of left kidney (reaches the hilum) and left crus of diaphragm
  • Medially: left celiac ganglion

Blood supply

Each gland receives three arteries and is drained by one suprarenal vein that leaves from the hilum.

Nerve supply

  • Sympathetic fibers from T8-L1.
  • Parasympathetic fibers from the vagus nerves.

Key Points / Quick Review

Structure

  • Cortex: glomerulosa (aldosterone), fasciculata (cortisol), reticularis (sex hormones)
  • Medulla: adrenaline and noradrenaline

Position

  • Epigastrium, in front of upper pole of each kidney; 4-5 gm
  • Right pyramidal, left semilunar; enclosed in Gerota's fascia

Supply

  • Arteries: inferior phrenic, abdominal aorta, renal artery
  • Veins: right to IVC, left to left renal vein
  • Nerves: sympathetic T8-L1; parasympathetic from vagus
